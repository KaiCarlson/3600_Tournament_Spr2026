Recommended Architecture
Here's what I'd actually build, in priority order:
Tier 1 (get to 70-80%):

A solid HMM rat tracker
A greedy carpet heuristic that correctly values carpet potential (not just current primed squares)
Time management: set a hard per-move time budget and fall back to a shallower search

Tier 2 (get to 80-90%):

Expectiminimax with alpha-beta pruning, depth 2-3
Incorporate rat EV into the heuristic (but outside the minimax tree — don't branch on 64 rat positions at every node, just add rat EV as a heuristic term)
Opponent modeling: penalize moves that gift long carpet lines to the opponent

Tier 3 (90%+):

Deeper search with iterative deepening (use your remaining time budget, not a fixed depth)
Better heuristic tuning — this is where empirical testing against yourself pays off
Consider whether the rat guess decision benefits from being in the tree vs. a separate policy
